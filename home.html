We are your connection to the Tampa Bay Trans Community

If you are from Tampa Bay (Hillsborough, Pinellas, Manatee counties, or others if you drive out here enough)
it's nice to meet you! We'll be listing ways to plug into the communtity soon in the mean time, feel free
to email us and we'll connect!

If your're from Florida but not Tampa, we'd love to set up neighborly lines of communitication!
If you'd like to list your resources on here, reach out to us. We are also interested in doing distribution
of any zines/newspapers/event fliers you'd like spread!

If you are from out of state, we come in peace! 👽 We want to build the network of trans solidarilty. If you know
any more projects like this, please help connect us. We are also interested in doing distribution
of any zines/newspapers/event fliers you'd like spread.
