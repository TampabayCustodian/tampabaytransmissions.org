Legal:

Floridanamechange.org

Step by step guide for changing your name in Florida. Spanish option available at top of website.

Southernlegal.org

Statewide not-for-profit public interest law firm. Assists individuals and groups with public interest issues who otherwise would not have access to the justice system and whose cases may bring about systemic reform

Medical:

DIY HRT Directory (Must be 18+)

DIY HRT Guides for Transmasc and Transfem individuals. Also includes guides/information on injection supplies.

Folxhealth.org

LGBTQIA+ centered healthcare platform.

Tbafund.org

FREE birth control, Plan B, Condoms and pregnancy strips.

gulfsouthplanb.com

Another resource for FREE birth control, Plan B, Condoms and pregnancy strips.

Topsurgery.net

Tool for finding a Top Surgery Surgeon in Florida.

The Evergreen Initiative

LGBTQ+ Affirming Mental Health Services, Education, and Consultation. Gender-Affirming Letters of support, Gender Affirming therapy.

SPARC Gynecology

Provides gynecological care (LGBTQIA+ Friendly), affirming care, and surgical care.

Basic needs:

TampaBay Mutual Aid

Facebook group for Mutual Aid.

Financial Aid:

Pointofpride.org

Financial aid for surgeries, electrolysis, HRT and other miscellaneous needs. Offers free Chest Binders and femme Shapewear.

Southernequality.org

Travel grants ($500, renewable) for Trans Youth (Under 18) impacted by Florida's SB254 law.
